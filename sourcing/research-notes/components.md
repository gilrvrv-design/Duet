## 4. 5V power note

- **Datasheet:** WorldSemi WS2812B Ver. No. V5 (world-semi.com footer), hosted by DigiKey at https://mm.digikey.com/Volume0/opasdata/d220001/medias/docus/8880/WS2812B.pdf (opened and text-extracted).
  - The LED characteristics table gives brightness at a "Working current" of **12 mA** per colour channel, with **quiescent current 0.6 mA**.
  - Supply range is VDD +3.7 to +5.3 V.
  - That works out to about 36 mA per LED at full white, plus 0.6 mA idle.
  - I found no official world-semi.com product page. Other hosted copies: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0174/1800/files/WS2812B-LED-datasheet.pdf and a V6 copy at https://www.tinytronics.nl/product_files/000438_WS2812B-V6_datasheet.pdf (neither opened).
- **Rule of thumb:** Adafruit uses **60 mA per LED** at full brightness (https://www.adafruit.com/product/1506). SuperLightingLED lists **43.2 W/m** for its 144/m strip, which is 8.64 A per metre.
- **For 180 LEDs at full white:**
  - Datasheet basis: 180 x 36.6 mA ≈ **6.6 A**.
  - Conservative (60 mA) basis: 180 x 60 mA ≈ **10.8 A**.
- **Supply:** A USB-C 5V supply gives up to 3 A. The Dig2Go page says it activates USB-C 5V 3A mode.
- **Brightness cap:** Keep roughly 0.3–0.5 A of headroom for the ESP32; this figure is unverified, so measure it. About 2.5 A is then left for LEDs, which is about **38% of full white on the datasheet basis** or **about 23% on the 60 mA basis**. Set the cap from the conservative figure, or measure the real strip. WLED has a current limiter, but I did not check its documentation page in this pass.
- **Voltage drop:** At 144/m, injecting power at both ends of 1.25 m is advisable. This is general practice and not sourced here.
