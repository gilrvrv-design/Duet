Tips
- Ask every supplier for prices at the same three quantities (100 / 500 / 1,000) so the replies drop straight into `../costs.xlsx`.
- Ask for prices **DDP USA** (delivered, duty paid) *and* **EXW/FOB**: DDP tells you the landed cost, FOB lets you compare with your own forwarder.
- Pay for samples through a platform with buyer protection (Alibaba Trade Assurance, PayPal) until a supplier has delivered once.
